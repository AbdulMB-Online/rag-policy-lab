# Information Security Policy v1.0

## SEC-01: Purpose and scope

Northstar Analytics protects company and customer information wherever employees
work. This policy applies to employees assigned to US offices and to their use
of company accounts, devices, and information during office work, approved remote
work, and business travel. A change in work location does not reduce security
requirements or authorize access to additional data.

Employees must use information only for approved business duties. The Remote Work,
Expense Reimbursement, and PTO policies remain responsible for scheduling,
reimbursement, and leave decisions. Security approval does not replace approvals
required under those policies.

## SEC-02: Roles and eligibility

Employees protect their accounts, follow handling rules, and report suspected
incidents. Managers request access appropriate to assigned duties and notify IT
Security when those duties change. The data owner, meaning the person accountable
for a dataset or business record, approves access and external sharing.

IT Security configures managed devices, maintains approved services, investigates
incidents, and records security exceptions. People Operations owns leave records;
Finance owns expense records. Access requires a documented business need and data
owner approval. A manager's position alone does not grant access to every
employee's personal information.

## SEC-03: Policy rules

Apply the classification and access requirements together. If information mixes
classifications, use the most protective applicable handling rule until the data
owner confirms an appropriate separation.

### SEC-03.1: Data classification and handling

SEC-T01: Classification examples and permitted handling

| Classification | Examples | Permitted handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Published job advertisements and approved marketing pages | Share externally only after the data owner approves public release. |
| Internal | Company policies and team availability calendars | Store in approved company services; share only with employees who need it for work. |
| Confidential | Customer records, itemized expense claims, and individual leave records | Use approved services with access restricted to named authorized recipients; obtain data owner approval before external sharing. |

The expense portal is the approved service for expense claims; the leave portal
is the approved service for individual leave records. Shared calendars may show
availability without private leave details. Redact unrelated account information
from expense evidence and exclude complete payment card numbers. If a
classification is unclear, ask the data owner before sharing.

### SEC-03.2: Devices and account access

Use company-managed devices for company work. IT Security enables device
encryption and automatic screen locking after five minutes of inactivity.
Employees must lock screens immediately when stepping away and must not disable
security settings. Personal devices and household accounts must not store or
process company information.

- Use individual company accounts and multifactor authentication for company
  services; never share passwords or approve unexpected authentication prompts.
- Connect through the company VPN when accessing internal network resources
  remotely. Use only services approved by IT Security for storage and transfer.
- Keep confidential conversations and screens inaccessible to visitors and
  household members. Do not forward company files to personal email accounts.

## SEC-04: Procedures

Report suspected phishing, lost devices, misdirected messages, or unauthorized
access immediately, without waiting for proof or manager approval:

1. Stop the affected activity. Do not open additional suspicious attachments,
   forward sensitive evidence, or attempt to investigate another person's account.
2. Report through the IT Security incident portal. If that portal is unavailable
   or inaccessible, telephone the IT help desk using the number in the company
   directory and request escalation to IT Security.
3. Provide the discovery time, affected account or device, a description, and
   the possible information involved. Never include passwords or authentication
   codes in a report.
4. Follow IT Security's containment instructions and preserve relevant messages
   and records. Do not delete evidence or notify external recipients without
   coordination with IT Security.

IT Security records the report, assigns an incident owner, and coordinates any
required follow-up with the data owner. Employees should also inform their
manager when an incident prevents normal work, without delaying the security
report.

## SEC-05: Exceptions and escalation

Request a security exception from IT Security before using an alternative
workflow. Include the business reason, affected information, proposed safeguards,
and requested end date. Any approval must be written, time-limited, and accepted
by the data owner. This exception process does not permit personal devices,
shared credentials, or bypassing multifactor authentication.

Escalate unresolved handling questions to the IT Security lead. Manager approval
alone never waives security controls or incident reporting.

## SEC-06: Ownership and review

IT Security owns this policy and reviews it annually with People Operations and
Finance. Data owners review access quarterly and request removal when business
needs end. IT Security retains access approvals, exception decisions, and incident
records in approved restricted systems and communicates approved policy changes
to employees.
