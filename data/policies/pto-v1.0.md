# PTO Policy v1.0

## PTO-01: Purpose and scope

Northstar Analytics provides paid time off so employees can take planned breaks
and manage personal needs while teams maintain coverage. This policy covers
discretionary PTO for regular, full-time employees assigned to US offices.
Company holidays, statutory leave, and separately administered medical leave are
outside this PTO bank. People Operations handles questions about those programs.

PTO represents time away from work. An approved remote day remains a working day
and does not consume PTO unless the employee actually takes leave. Working
remotely does not increase the PTO bank or create reimbursement eligibility under
the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## PTO-02: Roles and eligibility

Eligible employees begin earning PTO on their employment start date. Employees
check their available balance, request leave, and arrange a documented handoff.
Managers assess coverage and respond consistently to comparable requests.
People Operations maintains balances, resolves record discrepancies, and
coordinates questions about other leave programs.

Managers must not ask employees to disclose private medical details in a team
calendar or handoff document. Refer sensitive leave questions directly to People
Operations. Access to individual leave records must follow the Information
Security Policy, even when a team needs to know who is unavailable.

## PTO-03: Policy rules

The following rules govern the PTO bank and planned requests. A submitted request
does not reserve dates until the manager records approval.

### PTO-03.1: Accrual and balances

Eligible employees accrue 10 hours of PTO for each completed month of employment,
credited on the final calendar day of that month. A full calendar year of
employment therefore earns 120 hours. Employees may use credited hours immediately;
future accrual cannot be borrowed. PTO is recorded in one-hour increments against
the employee's scheduled working hours.

Up to 40 unused hours carry into the next calendar year. Hours above that amount
expire at year end. People Operations publishes each employee's available balance
in the leave portal. Employees must
report suspected errors before relying on a disputed balance for a request.

### PTO-03.2: Planned absence requirements

PTO-T01: Minimum notice and approval for planned absences

| Planned absence duration | Minimum notice before leave starts | Required approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 1 scheduled workday | 2 business days | Employee's manager |
| More than 1 and up to 5 consecutive scheduled workdays | 5 business days | Employee's manager |
| More than 5 consecutive scheduled workdays | 10 business days | Employee's manager and People Operations |

For this table, a business day is Monday through Friday excluding company
holidays. Measure duration in scheduled workdays; a partial workday counts as
the corresponding fraction of the employee's normal daily hours. Weekends and
company holidays between requested dates do not consume PTO. The notice periods
are minimums for planned requests; meeting them does not guarantee coverage or
approval.

## PTO-04: Procedures

Use this process for a planned absence:

1. Check the leave portal balance and select dates and scheduled hours. Submit
   the request within the notice period in PTO-T01, including a coverage plan.
2. The manager approves or declines within two business days of submission,
   recording a reason if coverage prevents approval. For longer absences, People
   Operations records its additional approval before the request is final.
3. After final approval, update the shared calendar with availability only and
   give colleagues the handoff information needed to cover active work.
4. Record actual leave hours in the portal. Request approval before extending or
   changing an absence; promptly cancel unused approved hours so the balance can
   be restored.

An unanswered request is not approval. Contact the manager for a decision before
the planned start date. Managers must not require routine work during approved
PTO; report any hours actually worked so the leave record can be corrected.

## PTO-05: Exceptions and escalation

For an unexpected absence, notify the manager as soon as practicable and contact
People Operations for guidance. The planned-notice table does not apply to
unexpected absences. People Operations determines whether PTO or another leave
program is appropriate and records any exception to routine procedures.

Escalate unresolved coverage disputes or balance errors to People Operations.
Insufficient PTO does not automatically authorize unpaid leave; obtain a separate
decision before assuming another arrangement is approved.

## PTO-06: Ownership and review

People Operations owns this policy, reviews it annually, and communicates approved
changes to managers and employees. Managers review coverage practices for
consistent application. People Operations retains approval and balance
corrections in the leave portal and resolves conflicting records before updating
an employee's available hours.
